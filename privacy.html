Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

PinkPath, LLC ("PinkPath," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy. This Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect information when you access or use the PinkPath mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

By accessing or using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and agree to the collection, use, and disclosure of information as described herein.

2. Information We Collect

A. Location Information

To provide navigation and safety-related features, we collect precise and approximate location information, including:

  • GPS coordinates while the app is in use
  • Starting points, destinations, and routes entered or navigated
  • Time-based location signals necessary to calculate safety scores

Location data is collected only while the app is active or when you expressly permit background location access.

B. Device and Technical Information

We may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Device type, model, and operating system
  • Browser type and version
  • IP address
  • Language preferences and time zone
  • App version and crash diagnostics

C. Usage and Interaction Data

We collect information about how you use the Services, such as:

  • Features accessed and frequency of use
  • Dates, times, and duration of sessions
  • Interaction patterns used to improve performance and usability

3. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Services
  • Generate route-specific safety scores and insights
  • Improve functionality, accuracy, and user experience
  • Conduct analytics and internal research
  • Communicate service-related notices and updates
  • Respond to inquiries, feedback, or legal obligations

PinkPath does not use collected data for targeted advertising.

4. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

PinkPath integrates third-party services to enable core functionality. These providers process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Maps Platform - Mapping, routing, and place search (Privacy Policy)
  • San Francisco Open Data (DataSF) - Crime and public safety datasets (Terms of Use)
  • Sunrise-Sunset.org - Daylight and solar timing data (API Info)

PinkPath does not sell or rent personal information. We may share limited data only when necessary to:

  • Provide requested services
  • Comply with legal obligations
  • Protect the rights, safety, and integrity of PinkPath and its users

5. Data Storage and Retention

A. Local Device Storage

Certain information is stored locally on your device for convenience and performance, including:

  • User preferences (e.g., display settings)
  • Recently searched locations
  • Cached route data

This information remains on your device unless you delete it or uninstall the app.

B. Retention Periods

  • Location and usage data are retained for no longer than 30 days, unless required for security, legal, or operational purposes
  • Aggregated and anonymized data may be retained indefinitely for analytics and product improvement

6. Security Measures

We employ reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect information, including:

  • Encryption of data in transit via HTTPS
  • Restricted access to sensitive systems
  • Regular security reviews and updates

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal information we hold
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete personal information
  • Opt out of certain data processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format

California Residents (CCPA/CPRA)

8. Children's Privacy

The Services are not intended for children under the age of 13. PinkPath does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take steps to delete it promptly.
