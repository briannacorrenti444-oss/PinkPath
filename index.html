How PinkPath Keeps You Safe

Crime Data Analysis

We continuously monitor local crime statistics from police departments and public safety databases to identify and avoid high-risk areas in real-time. Our algorithm updates every 15 minutes.

Routes are prioritized based on pedestrian activity data, ensuring you walk through populated, safer areas. More people around means increased safety and visibility.

We map municipal street lighting data and user reports to guide you through well-lit paths, especially important during evening and night hours.
